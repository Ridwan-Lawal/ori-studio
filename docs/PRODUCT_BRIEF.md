# ORÍ Studio — Product Brief

**Status:** Planning baseline | **Business:** Fictional natural-hair salon concept | **Geographic context:** Lagos, Nigeria

## Goal
Create a premium, compelling salon website demo that shows potential clients what a distinctive digital customer experience can look like. It must be useful, believable, accessible, responsive and clearly labeled a portfolio concept.

## Audience
- Potential salon visitors who want to see styles, understand the service offering and express appointment preferences.
- Business owners and decision-makers evaluating the creator's website-development ability.

## Problem hypothesis
Some salon visitors discover a business through social platforms but must search for services and styles across posts or send multiple messages to understand how to request an appointment. A dedicated site can organize that process. This is a design hypothesis, not a measured claim about any specific business.

## Value proposition
A visually memorable destination where a visitor discovers haircare services, explores a lookbook and previews a structured appointment request.

## Primary journey
Discover -> Explore services and looks -> Select service -> Choose preferred date and time -> Review a **demo** request summary.

## Product principles
1. Aesthetics serve customer clarity, not the reverse.
2. The salon's visual personality comes from photography and typography.
3. The primary flow should work comfortably on mobile.
4. Represent concept content honestly; do not imply real service operations.
5. Use low-friction interactions and provide recovery paths.
6. Build only the MVP needed to demonstrate product thinking and engineering quality.

## Working brand identity
- Name: ORÍ Studio, subject to commercial name-clearance for any real-world use.
- Category: Natural-hair care, protective styling and maintenance.
- Positioning: Contemporary African beauty editorial, premium but approachable.
- Voice: Specific, warm, concise and restrained.

## Success criteria (qualitative, no invented metrics)
- Visitors understand the fictional brand and service categories on the homepage.
- All relevant pages can be explored from desktop and mobile.
- The booking preview can be completed without a data submission or misleading confirmation.
- Accessibility, responsive layout, type clarity, imagery and performance are reviewed.
- A real working demo can be recorded for a 20–30 second social showcase.

## Not in MVP
Real scheduling/availability, payment, accounts, administrative tools, CMS, chatbot, persisted contact details, and business operations.
